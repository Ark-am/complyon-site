About the firm

Senior compliance expertise, delivered with precision.

ComplyOn LLC is an Atlanta-based independent compliance and risk advisory practice founded by Reema Keen, a compliance leader with more than sixteen years of experience building and overseeing regulatory compliance programs across SEC-registered investment advisers, institutional pension funds, broker-dealers, and global financial institutions.

Most recently, Reema served as Managing Director and Chief Compliance Officer at a $3 billion SEC-registered outsourced chief investment officer (OCIO) firm, where she institutionalized and enhanced the compliance program and brought the firm to full SEC examination readiness. Before that, she held senior compliance and operations leadership roles at UPS Investments Group, a $50B+ institutional pension fund, overseeing governance and due diligence across more than 200 investment managers. Earlier in her career, she supported enterprise-wide AML/BSA remediation following a DOJ consent order.

ComplyOn pairs senior-level judgment with a collaborative, scalable delivery model. Reema leads each engagement personally and works alongside clients' legal counsel, auditors, and administrators, drawing on trusted specialist partners and technology where a mandate calls for additional expertise or capacity. The aim is a long-term partnership: the right expertise, applied at the right scale, as each firm grows.

What we do

Compliance services for institutional complexity.

ComplyOn delivers senior advisory across the full compliance lifecycle — with the depth of an in-house CCO and the flexibility of an independent consultant.

SEC and state regulatory compliance

Examination readiness, Form ADV review, compliance program assessments, surveillance, conflicts of interest management, and regulatory filing support for registered advisers.

AML, OFAC & financial crime

Program design, gap assessments, OFAC screening frameworks, and enforcement action remediation support.

Operational due diligence

ODD frameworks for OCIO platforms and institutional allocators overseeing external managers, covering custody, AML, valuation, and third-party risk across investment managers spanning public, private, illiquid, and offshore fund structures.

Contract review & advisory

Compliance-focused review of NDAs, investment management and sub-advisory agreements, custodian agreements, and LPAs — identifying regulatory risk and governance gaps before execution.

Governance, risk & controls

Enterprise GRC framework design, board and committee governance review, cybersecurity policy development, control testing, escalation protocols, and board-level reporting infrastructure.

ERISA & DOL advisory

Governance and fiduciary compliance, prohibited transaction analysis, and operational due diligence frameworks for pension and institutional investment environments.

Fractional CCO services

Dedicated compliance leadership on a part-time basis — program ownership, compliance training, and regulatory interface, scaled to the size and stage of your organization.

Ongoing compliance support

Recurring support alongside your existing team — compliance calendar management, annual review and risk assessment, and advice on new or amended regulations, at whatever cadence your firm needs.

Digital asset frameworks

AML, custody, and safeguarding frameworks for investment advisers and funds with digital asset exposure, applying institutional compliance standards to requirements emerging under SEC, CFTC, and FinCEN rules and regulations.

Common questions

Answers before we connect.

Do you provide compliance services for broker-dealers?
No. ComplyOn focuses exclusively on investment advisers, OCIOs, pension funds, endowments and foundations, venture and emerging managers, and digital asset managers and funds. Reema's background includes broker-dealer compliance experience, but the firm does not currently offer broker-dealer compliance services.
Do regulators allow an outsourced or fractional Chief Compliance Officer?
Yes. The CCO may be in-house or outsourced, provided the individual is knowledgeable about applicable regulations and has sufficient authority and access to senior management to implement and enforce the compliance program.
What does an engagement cost?
It depends on scope. Fractional CCO engagements are billed as a monthly retainer, ongoing support as a retainer or hourly rate, and project-based work as an hourly or flat fee. A proposal follows an initial conversation about your firm and needs.
Do you work with firms that don't yet have a registered adviser or a formal compliance program?
Yes. ComplyOn supports firms building a compliance program from the ground up, including registration support, initial policy drafting, and governance infrastructure — as well as firms with mature programs seeking an experienced second set of eyes.
Can you support a firm with digital asset exposure?
Yes. ComplyOn works with investment advisers and funds with digital asset exposure, building AML, custody, and safeguarding frameworks that apply institutional compliance standards to requirements emerging under SEC, CFTC, and FinCEN rules and regulations.
How do you help firms keep pace with regulatory change?
Through ongoing compliance support or a defined project, ComplyOn tracks new and amended rules relevant to your firm, assesses their impact on your program, and helps implement the policy, procedural, and operational changes they require — before an examination tests them.
Do you work alongside other professionals?
Yes. Reema leads each engagement personally and works alongside a client's legal counsel, auditors, fund administrators, and technology providers, bringing in trusted specialist partners where an engagement calls for additional expertise or capacity. ComplyOn provides compliance consulting and does not provide legal, tax, or investment advice.
