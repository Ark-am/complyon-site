About ComplyOn LLC

Senior compliance expertise, delivered with precision.

ComplyOn LLC is an Atlanta-based independent compliance and risk advisory practice founded by Reema Keen, a compliance leader with more than 16 years of experience building and overseeing regulatory compliance programs across SEC-registered investment advisers, institutional pension funds, and global financial institutions.

Most recently, Reema served as Managing Director and Chief Compliance Officer at a $3.5 billion SEC-registered OCIO firm, where she institutionalized the compliance program from the ground up — leading Form ADV review, annual review audits, and custody audit coordination, and bringing the firm to full SEC examination readiness. Prior to that, she held senior compliance and operations leadership roles at UPS Investments Group, a $50B+ institutional pension fund, owning governance, operational due diligence, cybersecurity risk, and third-party risk frameworks across 200+ investment managers spanning single-manager and multi-manager platforms — experience that gave her particular fluency in the sub-adviser oversight and governance challenges facing manager-of-managers structures. Earlier in her career, she supported enterprise-wide AML/BSA remediation following a DOJ consent order.

While Reema's direct professional experience to date has been built entirely within traditional financial services, the competencies she has developed — AML/BSA program design, custody and safeguarding frameworks, enforcement action remediation, and institutional governance — form the regulatory foundation upon which digital asset compliance programs are built. As the industry matures under increasing SEC, FinCEN, and DOL oversight, Reema brings the institutional rigor and regulatory depth that emerging digital asset firms require as they build compliance infrastructure designed to withstand examination.

ComplyOn LLC brings institutional-grade rigor to organizations that need it — without the overhead of a large firm.

Common Questions

Answers before we connect.

Do you provide compliance services for broker-dealers?

No. ComplyOn LLC focuses exclusively on investment advisers, OCIOs, pension funds, and digital asset firms. While Reema's background includes broker-dealer compliance experience, ComplyOn does not currently offer broker-dealer compliance services.

Do regulators allow an outsourced or fractional Chief Compliance Officer?

Yes. The CCO may be in-house or outsourced, provided the individual is knowledgeable about applicable regulations and has sufficient authority and access to senior management to implement and enforce the compliance program.

What does an engagement cost?

It depends on scope. Fractional CCO engagements are billed as a monthly retainer, ongoing support as a retainer or hourly rate, and project-based work as an hourly or flat fee. A proposal follows an initial conversation about your firm and needs.

Do you work with firms that don't yet have an SEC-registered adviser or formal compliance program?

Yes. ComplyOn supports firms building a compliance program from the ground up, including registration support, initial policy drafting, and governance infrastructure — as well as firms with mature programs seeking an experienced second set of eyes.

Can you support a firm with digital asset exposure?

Yes. ComplyOn works with firms building AML, custody, and governance infrastructure for digital asset activities, applying institutional compliance frameworks developed across traditional financial services to the requirements emerging under SEC, FinCEN, and DOL oversight.
